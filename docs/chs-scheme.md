# CHS Scheme Reference

Summary of the Cedarcrest playbooks in `F:\CHSFootball\CHS playbook\` (O / D / ST), reviewed 2026-09-27.
The playbooks are Hudl exports made of diagrams. Most content comes from reading the drawings.
Offensive personnel and run concepts were confirmed by staff on 2026-09-27.

## Data layout

```
F:\CHSFootball\
  CHS playbook\O\    offense installs (formations/tags/motions, run, pass, screens)
  CHS playbook\D\    defense installs (2024–2026 books, coverage/front concept books)
  CHS playbook\ST\   special teams (PAT/FG, opponent-specific return plans)
  Opponents\<Team>\<Game folder>\   "Wide - Clip NNN.mp4" per play + breakdown .xlsx
```

## Offense

### Personnel letters
| Letter | Role |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | OL, numbered left to right (3 = center) |
| Q | QB (always gun) |
| T | Tailback |
| Y | FB/TE. The TE was moved off the line into a sidecar next to Q, on the side of the formation call (Trey Right = Y to the right of Q). Used in Deuce and Trey |
| X, Z, B | Receivers. B is usually the backside/single receiver |
| S | Slot receiver. Replaces Y in Doubles and Trio |

### Formations (all shown "Right"; flip for Left)
- **Deuce**: B wide backside, Z off-ball slot backside, X wide field; Y beside Q, T behind.
- **Trey**: B backside; Z and X to the call side; Y beside Q, T behind.
- **Doubles**: 2x2 (B + S, Z + X); T behind Q.
- **Trio**: 3x1 (Z, S, X to the call side), B backside.
- **Empty**
- **Tags:** Tight (B and X 5 yds from the tackles), Stacked, Inverted (slots on the LOS), Bunch.
- **Motions:** Zoom (Z across), Snake (S across), Orbit (Z behind Q), Thunder, Marker.

### Run game (city names; `Up`/`Down` = direction; number = hole)
- **Seattle** (Up/Down): true sweep, meant to reach the sideline
- **Jacksonville** (Up): toss/perimeter with jet or orbit motion and a Z crack
- **Chicago** (Up 21, Up 2, Down 46): counter with pullers
- **Tennessee** (2, 4): trap
- **Indy** (Up/Down 1, 2, 4, 5, 6): inside zone
- **Dallas** (Up, Up Chase, Up Insert): outside stretch. The RB cuts up inside only if his progression tells him to. Chase and Insert change Y's job

### Pass game
Cheetah, Delta, Mesh, Gator, Ram/Lion, Curl, Layers, Wolf (Read, Wrap), Dakota, Stick.
Red zone: Under, Bunch Whip Cross, 80 Marker. Play-action: 96 Up Dagger, 96 Up Yankee, 96 Up X-Orbit Yankee, 92 Down Ram.
Trio Bunch versions: Cheetah, Curl Poco, Dakota, Layers, Mesh, Ram.

### Screens
Key 1 / 2 / 3 (+ Wolf, Pop), Comet (Orbit / Thunder), Cougs Down, Top Gun, Detroit,
Deuce Left Marker Up Y Throwback ("Rose Star" call for the lineman).

## Defense

### Positions
E (strong end), T (3 tech), N (nose / 1 tech), B (weak/boundary end), S (Sam), M (Mike), W (Will), SS, FS, C, C.

### Strength rules (Rip = right, Liz = left)
- The TE side is strength. On motion, strength stays with the TE.
- No TE, 3x1: strength goes to the trips side.
- 2x2 in the middle of the field: strength goes left (the QB's passing arm).
- 2x2 on a hash: strength goes to the open (field) side.

### Fronts
Base Over, Stack (incl. Stack G), Under ("Utah"), Heavy, Bear, Tite, Shade, Odd (vs Sniffer), Even, Raven (Sam rolls down to a 7-tech "bang TE"), Goal Line Lock.

### Coverages (color codes)
- **Red** = Cover 1. "Lock" = man.
- **Blue** = Cover 2, plus a Tampa variant.
- **Green** = Cover 3.
- **Cover 4** (quarters).
- **Cover 6**: Cover 4 to the strong side, Cover 2 to the back side.

### Movements / pressures
Slant Strong "Slayer", Slant Weak "Sleak", Stack Not / Bronco / Tex / Ton, Badger, Mowhawk Lock, Fireman Lock, Sam Go, Mike Go, Gaps,
Trojan (4-man bogus pressure: shows single-high, plays Cover 2, Mike blitzes the strong B gap).

### Calls
- **Boys**: backers bracket the back.
- **Down**: Will rolls to the TE.
- **Move**: shifts the front.
- **W Gap Exchange has QB**
- **cloud / sky**: corner force vs safety force.
- **Peel**
- **You/Me**: goal-line motion exchange.

## Special teams
- **Kickoff return:** 5-2-2-2 (Left, Right, Middle; Richey; Fake Rev).
- **Punt return:** Return Right/Left, Block, Block Return Middle.
- **FG / PAT:** FG Fire.
- **Opponent-specific plans:** Foster 2025, Shorewood 2026, Monroe 2026, Renton 2026.
  - Foster 2025 is the baseline special teams plan for the 2026 Foster game.
  - Foster 2025 notes: "watch power squib", "watch bloop 1st", "middle most dangerous".

## Link to the opponent breakdowns
The Hudl breakdown `.xlsx` files describe the opponent in generic terms, for example OFF FORM "DOUBLES OPEN" or "PISTOL TRIPS OPEN" and OFF PLAY "FLY SWEEP" or "INSIDE ZONE READ".
These are the same terms our defensive install uses ("vs DOUBLES OPEN", "vs TRIPS OPEN", "vs GUN SPLIT"). That means opponent tendencies can be matched directly to the defensive answers in the playbook.
